Two terms: Agentor and AgentOrg

quukk defines two terms: an Agentor is a person who works through agents; an AgentOrg is an organization staffed by agentors and agents. With how the four products map to it.

Agentor 智人

A person who works through agents. Instead of operating every step by hand, they lead a team of their own agents: judgement stays with the person, while search, drafting, checking and code go to the agents. Two agentors arrive with their own conclusions and evidence, so one round is often enough.

AgentOrg 智能组织

An organization staffed by agentors and agents. Not layered by fixed posts, but assembled around the task at hand: execution, information exchange, process scheduling and archiving are separated, then held up by one shared identity and balance.
